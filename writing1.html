Writing Response 1

What is a ritual, practice or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why? How does this ritual or behavior contribute to your life? What aspect of it makes it valuable to you? What can it teach us about the nature or ingredients of human fulfillment more broadly?

Some routines that are important for my psychological wellbeing are morning walks, hot Pilates or hot vinyasa yoga, and drinking peppermint tea before bed. I like going on walks early in the morning when no one's really around, listening to music and singing along. I'm constantly thinking about something throughout the day, so during my walks I try not to think about anything and just empty my mind. I think it's a really good way to start my morning before everything gets busy.

I also really enjoy hot Pilates and hot vinyasa because of the physical challenge. I like how much I sweat during class because it makes me feel like I've really pushed myself, and I always leave feeling refreshed. But I also think it's really about mentality. A lot of the movements involve balance, and when you're thinking about something else, it's so easy to lose your balance. During more intense cardio sections, I always tell myself it's all about mentality and pushing through. It also helps me loosen my muscles and build strength, and I feel really fulfilled afterward knowing I made time to do something for myself on a busy day.

I also love drinking peppermint tea before bed. I've always loved peppermint flavored things, especially peppermint bark when I was younger, so it's nostalgic. I also like how it feels on my stomach and helps with digestion. My mom always used to make bedtime tea back home, so it reminds me of home too. Making tea is basically my way of telling myself that the day is over and it's time to go to bed.

I think what makes these routines valuable is that they give me time to myself when I'm usually thinking about so many other things. They let me think that fulfillment isn't always about being productive, but also about taking care of yourself, and making time for things you genuinely enjoy.
