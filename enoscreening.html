Media Response: Eno Screening

This is a media response for Eno, a generative documentary by Gary Hustwit.

It was interesting to watch how there were so many different interview videos and music, but the videography tied them all together.

How the documentary is unfinished and how more footage can be added as it goes was really cool.

The idea that something interactive doesn't have to be finished made me think differently about what a documentary or even a website can be.
