Media Response: There Are No Gardens on the Internet

This is a media response for There Are No Gardens on the Internet by Chia Amisola.

I really liked how interactive the website was, especially how clicking on different objects showed something new with little sound effects and music.

It felt more personal because I could explore things at my own pace instead of just scrolling through a website.

I want to incorporate something similar into my Shimmering Points website, using sounds and clickable objects to make the experience more fun to explore.
