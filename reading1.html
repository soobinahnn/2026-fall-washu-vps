Reading Response 1

This is a reading response for the conversation about restaurant websites from 2019.

Studying abroad in Florence made me notice how a lot of restaurant websites felt kind of old, with almost no structure in the typography and everything all over the place.

I usually like things that are more simple and elevated, but I actually liked how these websites felt more homey and traditional.

I also look at restaurant websites a lot when I’m traveling, so it made me realize that inconsistency can actually give a website more personality and tell you something about the place before you even go.
