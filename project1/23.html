The Microcreamery

A little about Clementine's

Clementine's is a St. Louis microcreamery making small-batch ice creams and sorbets locally.

They offer dairy-free, gluten-free, vegan, and alcohol-infused options. Gooey Butter Cake is one of their best-known flavors.

History

Since 2014

Ice cream changed her life. Twice.

Clementine's began with founder Tamara Keefe's childhood memory of making ice cream at home with a $2 hand-crank machine. Years later, that same idea of ice cream bringing people together inspired her to build a community of her own in St. Louis.

In 2014, Keefe invested her life savings and 401K into an FDA-certified ice cream kitchen and founded Clementine's.

She worked with food scientists and chemists to develop a process for freezing alcohol into ice cream, creating the signature “Naughty” flavors.

Today, Clementine's has grown to multiple parlors, nationwide shipping, and catering while keeping community at the center of the brand.
