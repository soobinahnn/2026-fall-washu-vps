# Clementine's Naughty & Nice Ice Cream

Small-batch ice cream and sorbet made in St. Louis.

Dairy-free • Gluten-free • Vegan • Alcohol-infused


## About Clementine's

Clementine's is a St. Louis microcreamery making small-batch ice creams and sorbets locally.

They offer dairy-free, gluten-free, vegan, and alcohol-infused options.

Gooey Butter Cake is one of their best-known flavors.
