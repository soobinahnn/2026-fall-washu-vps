Reading Response 4

This is a reading response for The Forbidden Zone by Damon Zucconi, Charles Broskoski, and Laurel Schwulst.

I related to how Damon talks about collecting things without knowing when they might become useful.

When I was working at an auction house this summer, I spent a lot of time looking through old catalogues and sometimes saved images just because I liked them.

I also save a lot of random photos and notes, and even though I rarely look back at the photos, I like rereading notes about little things that made me happy.
