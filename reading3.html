Reading Response 3

This is a reading response for My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be? by Laurel Schwulst.

This article felt personal to me because I recently started developing my portfolio for job applications, and I’ve mostly been using templates like Adobe Portfolio, which didn’t really satisfy me because it didn’t feel very personal.

If I made my own website, I think it could be like a shelf because I like things organized and knowing where everything goes, but still having little personal things around it.

Making it myself would let me organize everything in my own way and make it feel more like me.
