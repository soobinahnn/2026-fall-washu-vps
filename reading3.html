Reading Response 3

This is a reading response for My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be? by Laurel Schwulst.

I think my website could be like a shelf because I like when things feel organized and you know where everything goes.

But a shelf also has decorative things like a frame or a candle, so it still feels personal and not too perfect.

I usually like websites that are simple but have little playful details, and I like the idea that they can keep changing as your work and interests change.
