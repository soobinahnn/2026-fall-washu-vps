Writing Response 3

When do you cherish the slow or hard way of doing something? Why? When do you embrace constraints or prefer inefficiency? When do you willingly sacrifice convenience or comfort? What does the limited, slower, or more effortful approach offer that the quicker and easier way does not? What can we learn from this about human fulfillment and the kinds of technologies that might support it?

Sometimes I intentionally take the longer route to class, walking through the neighborhood and past Kaldi's instead of taking the quickest path. Especially during the fall, I love seeing all the trees and leaves changing colors because everything looks so pretty. Even though it's less efficient, I enjoy having that extra time to myself before class and noticing things I would otherwise miss. I think we're so used to technology showing us the fastest way to get somewhere, but sometimes I don't really care about saving those few minutes. I think the slower route can actually make an ordinary part of my day more enjoyable.
