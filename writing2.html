Writing Response 2

It could be an object, tool, work of art, process, system, environment, or something else human-made. What positive qualities does this creation call forth in you? What positive state(s) does it awaken in you? How does it empower you to express your value and strengths? Why does it have this effect on you? Rather than focusing on tools that augment your productivity or performance on instrumental tasks, pay attention to how human-made things uplift you and allow you to be the best version of yourself.

I would choose Do Ho Suh's Walking House. Having lived in different cities like Seoul, St. Louis, Wellington, London, and New York, I feel like the idea of home has always been changing for me. Sometimes it feels unstable because I'm constantly moving, but I also realized that the longer I stay in a place, the more comfortable and familiar it becomes, and maybe that's what makes somewhere feel like home. I like how the house in his work is constantly walking, because it reminds me that home doesn't necessarily have to be one fixed place. It gives me a sense of reassurance that even if I move somewhere unfamiliar, I can eventually find comfort there. I think this makes me less scared of moving or trying new things, and reminds me to be more adaptable to different environments rather than worrying too much about where I belong.
