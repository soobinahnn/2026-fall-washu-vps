Reading Response 2

This is a reading response for “The New Turing Test” by Elan Ullendorff.

What resonated with me was the idea that design should feel like it came from someone and was made for someone.

It reminded me of a professor asking us what separates art from design, and I feel like design always has some kind of purpose or specific audience behind it.

I use AI sometimes for things like mockups after I’ve already made something, but not really for the actual brainstorming or design process because I think being intentional is such a big part of it.

It also made me wonder if more generic corporate or web design could eventually be replaced by AI since a lot of it already feels pretty impersonal.
